Privacy policy

J Fox Studio · Last updated November 13, 2026

J Fox Studio is a personal app used only by its owner, Joseph Miller. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app reads his basic profile, account statistics (followers, likes, video count) and his public video list with view, like, comment and share counts. These are used only to show engagement inside the app.

Where data is stored

TikTok access tokens and app settings are stored encrypted on the owner's phone using the Android Keystore. The post log is stored on the phone and backed up to the owner's private GitHub repository. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party, other than the AI service the owner uses to write hooks and hashtags.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect TikTok in the app's settings or revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security, Manage app permissions.

Questions: open an issue on github.com/jmiller-sec.
